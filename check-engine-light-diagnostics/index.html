Mobile Check Engine Light Diagnostics

A code reader tells you where to look. It doesn't tell you what's broken. A P0171 "system too lean" code, for example, can come from a vacuum leak, a dirty MAF sensor, a weak fuel pump, or a cracked intake boot, and each fix costs something very different.

I bring the scan tool to your driveway or parking lot, reads the codes and live data, and then does the actual troubleshooting: checking connectors, testing sensors, looking for leaks. You get a plain-English explanation of what's wrong and what it will cost before you spend anything on parts.

What's included

  • Full code scan, including pending and history codes where your vehicle supports them
  • Live data review (fuel trims, sensor readings, misfire counts)
  • Visual inspection of wiring, connectors, hoses and vacuum lines related to the code
  • A clear explanation of the likely cause and what I recommend
  • An upfront quote for the repair, whether that's done on the spot or scheduled

Signs you need it

  • The check engine light is on steady
  • The light is flashing (usually an active misfire; drive as little as possible)
  • Rough idle, hesitation, or loss of power
  • Worse fuel mileage than usual
  • A failed emissions/state inspection
  • A smell of fuel or rotten eggs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to keep driving with the check engine light on?

If the light is steady and the car drives normally, it's usually OK to drive gently until it's checked. If it's flashing, or the car is running rough, overheating or losing power, stop driving it and call. A flashing light often means a misfire that can damage the catalytic converter.

Can't I just get the code read for free at a parts store?

You can, and it's a fine first step. But a code is a starting point, not a diagnosis. I test the parts behind the code so you're not replacing sensors on a guess.

Will clearing the code fix the problem?

No. Clearing a code just turns the light off until the computer sees the fault again. It also erases readiness data you need to pass inspection.
