Why Walter

Hi, I'm Walter Curo. I learned to fix vehicles in the U.S. Army, where I served four years and was honorably discharged as a Specialist (E-4). As an Army mechanic I led preventive and corrective maintenance on more than 200 vehicles and pieces of equipment, and I trained and supervised junior maintainers. In the Army, things have to work every time. I still work that way.

After the Army I spent years in dealership collision center work, writing estimates and seeing what goes right and wrong with cars. Before that I was an insurance auto damage adjuster, so I know how claims work and can explain it in plain English.

I started Curo My Car because getting your car fixed shouldn't wreck your day. I come to you, I tell you what's wrong, and I tell you the price before I start. No runaround.
