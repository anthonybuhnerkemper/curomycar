Why Walter

Hi, I'm Walter Curo, owner of Curo My Car. I grew up around cars, watching my family work on them. My formal training came from the U.S. Army, where I served as a mechanic. In the Army, things have to work every time. I still work that way.

After the Army I worked on big diesel trucks and heavy seismic vibrator trucks, then forklifts and propane-fueled engines. As a dealership technician I did everything from oil changes to engine swaps, engine teardowns and frame work.

Then I spent years as an auto insurance adjuster and in the body-shop world, learning how vehicles are built, structure and all, across every make and model. Other mechanics came to me when they were stumped. Today I work on all makes and models. If it's on your car, just ask.

I started Curo My Car because getting your car fixed shouldn't wreck your day. I come to you, I tell you what's wrong, and I tell you the price before I start. No runaround.

Ask me about current payment options when you book.
