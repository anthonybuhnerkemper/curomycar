On-Site Fleet Maintenance for Small Businesses

Every hour a work truck spends at a shop is an hour it isn't on a job. For contractors, landscapers, delivery services and other small businesses around Fort Bend County, on-site maintenance keeps vehicles in service and drivers working.

I trained as a mechanic in the Army, then worked on big diesel trucks, heavy equipment and forklifts. The same discipline applies to a small fleet: regular service, records you can rely on, and catching problems before they strand a crew.

What's included

  • Scheduled oil changes and fluid service at your yard or office
  • Brake, tire, belt and hose inspections
  • Battery and charging system testing
  • Diagnostics when a vehicle throws a warning light
  • Simple service records per vehicle
  • Coordinated scheduling so vehicles are serviced around your workday

Signs you need it

  • Your trucks or vans lose work days waiting at a shop
  • Maintenance is tracked on memory instead of a schedule
  • Breakdowns are happening that inspections would have caught
  • Drivers are taking time off the clock to drop vehicles off
