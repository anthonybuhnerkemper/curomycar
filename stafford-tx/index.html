Car repair that comes to you in Stafford

Stafford packs a lot into a small footprint: homes and apartments off Kirkwood Road and Murphy Road, business parks and warehouses along the Southwest Freeway (US-59/I-69) and West Airport Boulevard, and the busy stretch around Stafford Centre and the Houston Community College campus. A lot of Stafford drivers commute on US-59 or Beltway 8 every day, and that stop-and-go traffic is hard on brakes, batteries and cooling systems.

Mobile service fits Stafford well. If you work in one of the office or industrial parks near the freeway, Walter can service your car in the lot while you're at your desk (with your employer's permission). If you're at home, he works in your driveway.
