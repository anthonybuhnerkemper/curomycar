Mobile Tune-Up Service

Modern cars don't need the old-school tune-up every few months, but they still have parts that wear out on a schedule: spark plugs, ignition coils, and air filters. Letting them go costs you in fuel mileage, smooth running, and sometimes a check engine light.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do spark plugs need replacing?

It depends on the engine and plug type. Many modern iridium plugs are rated for long intervals. Your owner's manual has the number.

What's the difference between the engine and cabin air filter?

The engine air filter cleans air going into the engine. The cabin filter cleans the air you breathe inside the car. Both get dirty faster in dusty or high-pollen conditions.

Will a tune-up fix my check engine light?

Only if the cause is a worn plug or coil. I check the code first so you're not paying for parts you don't need.
