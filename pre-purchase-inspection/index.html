Mobile Pre-Purchase Used Car Inspection

A used car can look perfect in photos and still hide problems: a past accident, an engine with stored codes that were just cleared, worn suspension, or brakes on their last legs. A pre-purchase inspection gives you an independent second opinion before money changes hands.

My background is a real advantage here. Beyond my Army mechanic training and dealership work, I spent years as an auto insurance adjuster and in the body-shop world, learning how vehicles are built, so I know what poorly repaired accident damage looks like: mismatched paint, uneven panel gaps, overspray, and replaced parts that don't line up.

What's included

  • Exterior and body check for signs of prior collision repair or flood exposure
  • Under-hood inspection for leaks, belts, hoses and fluid condition
  • Computer scan for stored, pending and recently cleared codes
  • Brakes, tires, steering and suspension check
  • Lights, AC, electrical accessories and warning lights
  • Test drive (with the seller's permission)
  • A plain-English rundown of what I found so you can negotiate or walk away

Signs you need it

  • You're buying from a private seller
  • The car is from a lot without a solid inspection report
  • The price seems too good
  • The vehicle history is unclear or shows an accident
  • You're buying for a new driver and want peace of mind

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you tell if a car has been in an accident?

Often, yes. My adjuster and body-shop background means I know where to look for repaired damage. No inspection can guarantee to catch everything, but it catches a lot.

Do I need to be there?

It's helpful but not required. I can walk you through what I found by phone afterward.

Is a vehicle history report enough?

It's useful, but it only shows what was reported. Plenty of damage is fixed without an insurance claim. A hands-on inspection fills that gap.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle. Call or text and I'll give you an estimate.
