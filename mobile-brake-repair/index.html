Mobile Brake Repair at Your Home or Work

Brakes are the one system you can't afford to put off. The good news: most pad and rotor jobs don't need a shop. Walter can do them in your driveway or an office parking lot while you get on with your day.

Call (281) 789-8609 Text Get a Quote

Every brake job starts with an inspection, so you only pay for what the car actually needs. If your rotors still have life in them, he'll say so.

What's included

  • Brake inspection: pad thickness, rotor condition, calipers, hardware and brake hoses
  • Front and/or rear brake pad replacement
  • Rotor replacement when they're worn past spec, warped or scored
  • Cleaning and lubricating slide pins and contact points
  • Brake fluid level and condition check
  • A test drive to confirm the brakes feel right

Signs you need it

  • High-pitched squeal when braking (many pads have a built-in wear indicator)
  • Grinding or metal-on-metal noise
  • Steering wheel or pedal vibration when stopping
  • Car pulls to one side under braking
  • Soft, spongy or low brake pedal
  • Brake warning light on the dash

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I always need new rotors with new pads?

No. Rotors get replaced when they're below minimum thickness, warped, or badly grooved. Walter measures and tells you what he finds.

How long do brake pads last?

It varies a lot with driving. Stop-and-go traffic on the Southwest Freeway or Highway 6 wears pads faster than highway miles. An inspection is the only way to know.

Is grinding an emergency?

Grinding usually means the pad material is gone and metal is hitting the rotor. Get it looked at right away. It's less safe and it gets more expensive the longer it goes.

Do you use quality parts?

Walter will tell you what parts he plans to use and why, and the price, before any work starts.
